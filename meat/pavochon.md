# Pavochón (Puerto Rican Roast Turkey)

Serves about 10. Marinate overnight or up to 2 days.

## Ingredients

### Turkey

- 1 whole turkey (12 to 15 pounds), fully thawed
- 2 tablespoons annatto oil (aceite de achiote)
- 2 teaspoons adobo seasoning, for the skin

### Marinade

- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil or avocado oil
- 1/4 cup distilled white vinegar
- 1/4 cup sofrito
- 2 heads garlic, cloves peeled and crushed
- 2 tablespoons chicken bouillon paste
- 2 tablespoons adobo seasoning
- 2 tablespoons sazón seasoning
- 1 1/2 tablespoons ground oregano
- 1 1/2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- Up to 1 1/2 teaspoons salt, depending on the saltiness of the seasonings

### Cavity aromatics

- 1 onion, quartered
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 carrots, halved
- 2 celery ribs, halved
- 1 lemon, quartered
- 10 to 15 cilantro sprigs

### Roasting pan

- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1/4 cup chicken or turkey broth
- 1/4 cup fresh orange juice or sour orange juice

## Instructions

1. Remove the neck and giblets and pat the turkey dry. Stir the marinade ingredients together, adjusting the salt before the marinade touches the turkey.
2. Loosen the skin with your fingers and spread the marinade over and under it, including over the breast and thighs. Rub some inside the cavity. Cover and refrigerate overnight or up to 2 days.
3. Heat the oven to 350°F. Loosely fill the cavity with the aromatics and set the turkey breast-side up in a roasting pan. Rub the skin with annatto oil and sprinkle with the 2 teaspoons of adobo. Pour the wine, broth, and juice into the pan. Seal the pan with foil.
4. Roast for 2 hours, then baste with the pan juices and re-cover. Allow roughly 3 to 4 hours total and start checking the temperature after 2 1/2 hours. Uncover for the last 20 to 30 minutes to brown the skin. Shield any spots that darken early with foil.
5. Roast until the thickest part of the breast and the innermost parts of the thigh and wing each reach at least 165°F, measuring away from bone. Rest for 30 minutes, then remove the cavity aromatics and carve. [USDA turkey doneness guidance](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/What-is-the-internal-temperature-and-cooking-time-for-turkey)

## Notes

- For a frozen turkey, allow 3 to 4 days to thaw it in the refrigerator, plus time to marinate. [USDA refrigerator thawing guidance](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/How-long-can-a-thawed-turkey-remain-in-the-refrigerator-after-thawing)
- Roast with the aromatics in the cavity and bake [Herb Stuffing](../sides/herb-stuffing.md) separately if serving it. [USDA turkey and stuffing guidance](https://www.nifa.usda.gov/sites/default/files/resource/Serving%20Turkey%20Safely.pdf)
- For gravy, strain and skim the pan drippings. Bring 1 cup of drippings to a simmer, then whisk in 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons cold water. Simmer until thickened.
