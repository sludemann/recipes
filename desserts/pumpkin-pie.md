# Pumpkin Pie

## Ingredients

- 1 unbaked 9-inch deep-dish pie crust
- 2 cups plain pumpkin purée
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 4 large eggs

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F with a rack in the lower third. Fit the crust into a 9-inch deep-dish pie plate, crimp the edge, and chill while the oven heats.
2. Line the crust with parchment and fill it with pie weights or dried beans. Bake for 15 minutes, then lift out the parchment and weights. Leave the oven at 400°F.
3. Whisk the cinnamon, ginger, allspice, cloves, and nutmeg into the heavy cream in a small saucepan. Heat over medium-low, stirring occasionally, until the cream just starts to steam. Remove from the heat, cover, and let the spices steep for 5 minutes.
4. Whisk the pumpkin, brown sugar, maple syrup, and milk together. Whisk in the spiced cream. Once the mixture is lukewarm, whisk in the eggs just until smooth.
5. Set the pie plate on a rimmed baking sheet and pour the filling into the crust. Bake for about 40 minutes, checking at 35 minutes. The filling should be set around the edges and jiggle gently in the center when you nudge the pan. If using an instant-read thermometer, aim for 160–170°F at the center. Cover the crust edge with foil if it browns before the filling sets.
6. Cool on a wire rack for about 2 hours, then serve or refrigerate.

## Notes

- The filling continues to set as it cools. Use the center's jiggle to judge doneness, since the bake time can vary with the pan and oven. [King Arthur Baking’s pumpkin pie doneness guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2024/09/25/how-to-tell-when-pumpkin-pie-is-done)
