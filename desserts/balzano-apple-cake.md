# Apple Cake (Torta Bolzano)

Serves 8.

## Ingredients

- 8 tablespoons (113 g) salted butter
- 12 ounces Granny Smith apples
- 12 ounces Honeycrisp or Gala apples
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 161 g (about 3/4 cup) granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup (130 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon fine table salt
- 2/3 cup whole milk
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon turbinado or granulated sugar, for topping
- Powdered sugar, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease a 9-inch round cake pan, line the base with parchment, and grease the liner.
2. Swirl the butter in a saucepan over medium-high heat until the solids darken and smell nutty, 3–5 minutes. Transfer to a bowl to cool.
3. Peel and core the apples. Slice them 1/16 inch thick and toss with the lemon juice.
4. Beat the granulated sugar, eggs, and zest by hand for about a minute, until lighter and thicker. Whisk in the flour, baking powder, and salt, then slowly stir in the milk, browned butter, and vanilla.
5. Coat the apples with the batter. Spread and press them flat in the pan, then tap it on the counter to settle the batter. Scatter the topping sugar over the apples. Bake on the middle rack for 55–60 minutes, until deeply golden.
6. Rest in the pan for 15 minutes. Loosen the edge, turn out onto a rack, peel away the liner, and flip upright onto a platter. Cool fully before dusting with powdered sugar.

## Notes

- Weigh the apples before peeling. Stick to 1 1/2 pounds total so the cake sets properly.
