# Thanksgiving Shopping List

Plan on one 12-pound whole turkey for 6–10 people and one full batch of each other recipe. The checklist uses the Stove Top stuffing variation, French’s fried onions, two homemade pie crusts, prepared cocktail sauce and veggie dip, and homemade cranberry sauce.

## Meat and seafood

- [ ] 1 whole turkey, about 12 lb
- [ ] 2 lb cooked, peeled large shrimp for shrimp cocktail
- [ ] Pack of smoked Bacon for crumbling

## Produce

- [ ] 1 veggie tray, about 2 lb of cut vegetables, for dipping
- [ ] 2 lb fresh green beans
- [ ] 1 1/2 lb white button mushrooms
- [ ] Broccoli crowns or florets, enough for 8 cups of florets (about 2 lb of florets)
- [ ] 3 yellow onions, including 2 large ones for stuffing
- [ ] 1 red onion
- [ ] 3 large heads of garlic, enough for 2 whole heads plus 8 cloves
- [ ] 1 bunch celery
- [ ] 2 carrots for the turkey
- [ ] 1 bunch cilantro
- [ ] 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
- [ ] Fresh sage, rosemary, and thyme, 1 small bunch or pack each
- [ ] 3 lemons for the turkey, casserole, cake, and shrimp
- [ ] 1 large orange for the turkey, or enough orange juice for 1/4 cup
- [ ] 12 oz Granny Smith apples
- [ ] 12 oz Honeycrisp or Gala apples
- [ ] 1 bag (12 oz) fresh cranberries for cranberry sauce

## Dairy and eggs

- [ ] 1 lb unsalted butter, kerry gold
- [ ] 1 box salted butter, kerry gold. for the apple cake
- [ ] 1 quart heavy cream (2 1/4 cups needed)
- [ ] 1 pint whole milk (1 1/6 cups needed)
- [ ] 1 tub prepared veggie dip, about 16 oz

## Bread, canned goods, and condiments

- [ ] 1 loaf crusty white bread (10 oz needed for the Stove Top variation)
- [ ] 1 box (6 oz) Stove Top Turkey Stuffing Mix
- [ ] French’s Crispy Fried Onions, enough for 2 cups
- [ ] 2 cartons (32 fl oz each) unsalted chicken stock or broth (about 5 cups needed, including extra to adjust the stuffing)
- [ ] 1 bottle cocktail sauce, about 12 oz
- [ ] 1 jar pickles
- [ ] 1 jar olives
- [ ] Dukes Mayo
- [ ] Sofrito (1/4 cup)
- [ ] Chicken bouillon paste (2 tablespoons)
- [ ] Dry white wine (1/4 cup)

## Baking and pantry check

- [ ] All-purpose flour (590 g, plus extra for rolling the crusts)
- [ ] Granulated sugar (about 1 lb needed, including cranberry sauce and the cake topping)
- [ ] Brown sugar (1 1/3 cups packed)
- [ ] Powdered sugar for the cake
- [ ] Vegetable shortening (1/4 cup)
- [ ] Pecan halves (2 cups)
- [ ] Dried cranberries (1/2 cup)
- [ ] Sunflower seeds (1/4 cup)
- [ ] Light corn syrup (1 cup)
- [ ] Maple syrup (2 tablespoons)
- [ ] Unsulfured molasses (1 tablespoon)
- [ ] Vanilla extract (5 teaspoons)
- [ ] Baking powder (1 teaspoon)
- [ ] Apple cider vinegar (3 tablespoons plus 2 teaspoons)
- [ ] Distilled white vinegar (1/4 cup)
- [ ] Soy sauce (2 teaspoons)
- [ ] Olive or avocado oil, canola oil, and annatto oil
- [ ] Adobo seasoning, sazón, ground oregano, onion powder, and garlic powder
- [ ] Ground cinnamon, ginger, allspice, cloves, and nutmeg
- [ ] Fine salt, kosher salt, and black pepper
- [ ] Cornstarch if making gravy from the turkey drippings
- [ ] Parchment, foil, and pie weights or dried beans if needed

## Recipe swaps

- For [from-scratch stuffing](sides/herb-stuffing.md), use 1 lb of crusty bread and add 4 tablespoons unsalted butter to the shopping total. The fresh herb packages above cover either version.
- For purchased crusts, buy two unbaked 9-inch deep-dish shells. The [homemade crusts](desserts/pie-crust.md) account for 400 g of the flour, 16 tablespoons of the unsalted butter, all the shortening, 2 tablespoons of granulated sugar, and 2 teaspoons of the cider vinegar.
