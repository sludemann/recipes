# Corn Pudding

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons packed brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons plain Greek yogurt or sour cream
- 1/2 cup butter, melted and slightly cooled, plus more for greasing the dish
- 1/2 cup milk
- 3 large eggs
- 1 can (about 15 ounces) cream-style corn
- 1 can (about 15 ounces) whole-kernel corn, rinsed and well drained
- 1 box Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Grease a 2-quart baking dish.
2. Whisk the eggs, granulated sugar, brown sugar, and Greek yogurt or sour cream in a large bowl. Whisk in the milk and melted butter, then stir in both kinds of corn.
3. Stir in the corn muffin mix just until no dry pockets remain. Pour the batter into the prepared dish and spread it evenly.
4. Bake for 40 to 50 minutes, until the top is golden and the center is set. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving warm.
