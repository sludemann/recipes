# Mashed Potatoes

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 1 1/2-inch chunks
- 1 small yellow onion, peeled and quartered
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- Salt, for the cooking water and to taste

## Instructions

1. Put the potatoes, onion, and garlic in a pot and cover with cold water by about an inch. Salt the water, bring to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer. Cook for 15 to 20 minutes, until the potatoes and onion are very tender.
2. Drain well and set the onion and garlic aside. Let the potatoes steam dry in the colander for a minute or two, then press them through a potato ricer into a large bowl while still hot.
3. Smash the onion and garlic together with a fork. If the onion leaves long strands, chop them before adding the mixture to the potatoes.
4. Add the onion and garlic, butter, and salt to the hot riced potatoes. Stir gently until the butter melts and everything is evenly distributed. Taste and adjust the salt, then serve warm.
