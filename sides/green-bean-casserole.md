# Green Bean Casserole

Serves 8 to 12.

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds fresh green beans, trimmed and cut into 2-inch lengths
- 1 1/2 pounds white button mushrooms, cleaned, dried, and roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons canola oil
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken stock
- 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
- 2 cups French’s Crispy Fried Onions, divided

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Boil the beans in salted water for about 5 minutes, until bright green and crisp-tender. Cool them in ice water, then drain well.
2. Heat the oil and butter in a wide skillet over high heat. Cook the mushrooms, stirring occasionally, until their liquid cooks off and they begin to brown, 6 to 10 minutes.
3. Lower the heat to medium-high. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds, then the flour for 1 to 2 minutes. Gradually whisk in the stock, cream, soy sauce, and lemon juice. Simmer until the sauce lightly coats a spoon, about 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Toss the beans with the sauce and 1 cup of the fried onions. Spread in a 9-by-13-inch baking dish and bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until bubbling. Scatter the remaining onions over the casserole just before serving.

## Notes

- **Homemade fried shallots.** Start 1 pound of peeled shallots, sliced 1/8 inch thick, in 2 cups of cold canola oil in a saucepan. Cook over high heat, stirring frequently and then constantly as they color, until pale gold, about 20 minutes. Strain into a heatproof bowl, spread the shallots on paper towels, salt lightly, and let cool. Use the roughly 2 cups of shallots in place of the fried onions and 2 tablespoons of the frying oil in the mushroom sauce.
