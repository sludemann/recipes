# Broccoli Salad

Serves 8.

## Ingredients

- 8 cups fresh broccoli florets, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1/3 cup diced red onion
- 1/2 cup dried cranberries
- 1/4 cup sunflower seeds
- 1/2 cup cooked, crumbled bacon or real bacon bits

### Dressing

- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Rinse the broccoli and pat it dry so it stays crisp and the dressing stays creamy.
2. Whisk the mayonnaise, vinegar, and sugar until smooth, then season with salt and pepper.
3. Put the broccoli, onion, cranberries, sunflower seeds, and bacon in a large bowl. Add the dressing and toss until the florets are evenly coated.
4. Cover and chill for at least 1 hour. Toss again before serving.

## Notes

- You can dress the salad up to a day ahead. For crisp bacon on top, save a spoonful to scatter over the salad when you serve it.
- Adapted from [Holly Nilsson’s Broccoli Salad at Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/broccoli-salad/).
